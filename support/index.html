FAQ

What is CIS Tracker for? It helps UK construction subcontractors paid under the Construction Industry Scheme log payments and CIS deductions per contractor and see totals for each tax year.

Which deduction rates does it support? The standard CIS rates: 20% for registered subcontractors, 30% for those not registered, and 0% for gross payment status.

What tax year does the dashboard use? The UK tax year, 6 April to 5 April.

Is the refund figure accurate? It is an estimate only and is not tax advice. Your actual position depends on your full circumstances. Check with HMRC or a qualified accountant.

Where is my data stored? Only on your device. There is no account, no tracking and no third-party analytics.

How do I get my records to my accountant? Export to CSV or PDF, or create a PDF statement, then share it using the iOS share sheet.

How do I set or stop reminders? Turn weekly reminders on or off in the app's settings, or in iOS Settings > Notifications.

How much does it cost? £2.99 per month or £19.99 per year, with a 7-day free trial.

How do I cancel my subscription? iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > CIS Tracker. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

I got a new phone. Where is my data? Data lives on the device. It moves to a new phone if you restore from a backup of the old one.

How do I restore my purchase? Open the subscription screen and tap Restore Purchases.
