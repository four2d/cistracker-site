CIS Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

CIS Tracker ("the app") is made by Pete Dent. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

CIS Tracker does not collect, transmit or sell your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data you enter

Contractor names, payment amounts, materials, labour, CIS deductions and any notes are stored only on your device. We have no server and cannot see this data.

Tracking and analytics

The app contains no tracking, advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Exports and statements

When you create a PDF statement or a CSV/PDF export, the file is created on your device. It is only shared if you choose to share it (for example by email or AirDrop), and then it is handled by the app or service you choose.

Notifications

Weekly reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Backups

Your device's own backups (such as iCloud Backup, if you have it turned on) may include the app's data. These are controlled by Apple and your device settings.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app, or delete the app to remove all its data from your device.

Children

The app is not aimed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
